FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Good questions, straight answers.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

We accept small and large orders. MOQs are product-specific and depend on garment, fabric, colours and decoration method. Share your requirements and we confirm the MOQ in your quote.

Can you manufacture under my own brand name?

Yes. Private label is a core service: your logo, neck labels, hang tags and packaging.

Do you make samples before bulk production?

Yes. Sample cost and timing depend on the product and are confirmed during your consultation.

Do I need a tech pack?

It helps us quote accurately but is not required. Reference images, sketches or a sample garment are enough to start.

What printing and embroidery methods do you offer?

Screen printing, DTG, DTF and embroidery, where available for your fabric and design.

How do I request an accurate quote?

Send the product type, fabric, quantity, size range, branding, packaging, destination and target date by WhatsApp or the quote form.

Do you export internationally?

Yes. Orders are packed and prepared for international shipment. Shipping method and terms are agreed in your quotation.

Still have a question? Email glamtiqueapparels@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp.

Ready to start your collection?

Send a sketch, a reference photo or a full tech pack. We reply with options and a quotation.
