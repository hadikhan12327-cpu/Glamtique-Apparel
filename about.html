About Glamtique Apparels

A clothing manufacturer and exporter based in Sialkot, Pakistan.

A manufacturing partner for growing brands

We help clothing brands, fashion startups, streetwear labels and growing businesses turn ideas into finished, branded garments.

From custom hoodies and oversized t-shirts to tracksuits, gym wear, yoga wear, denim and sportswear, we handle development, sampling, production, branding and packing, so you work with one team instead of many suppliers.

Sialkot has a long reputation for sportswear and apparel manufacturing, and we are proud to build on it, with clear communication at every step.
